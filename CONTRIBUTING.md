# Maintenance Policy

`terminal-setup` is independently maintained by its author. It is published as a complete reference implementation, not as a community-maintained product.

The maintainer does not accept pull requests, feature requests, support requests, configuration requests, or roadmap proposals. There is no contribution workflow or response-time commitment.

You may fork the repository under the MIT License and maintain a variant that matches your own workflow. Please keep credentials, machine-specific state, and personal infrastructure out of public forks.

Suspected credential exposure or a security vulnerability is the only exception. Follow the private reporting guidance in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
